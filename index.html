Ridgeline

Find where a landscape photo was taken: the skyline in your picture is matched against the real hills and mountains.

Opening Ridgeline ...
Open Ridgeline

If it has been asleep it shows "This app has gone to sleep": tap the button there and wait about a minute.

Put it on your home screen

iPhone or iPad (Safari): tap the Share button (square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android (Chrome, Samsung Internet, Edge): tap the three dots, then Add to home screen or Install app.

Then open it from the mountain-and-pin icon Ridgeline: it goes straight to the app.

In the app, "From this device" opens your photo library. Choose the original photo, not a screenshot, so the lens information is kept.

Free, no ads, no account. Terrain from open elevation data (AWS Terrain Tiles); maps from OpenTopoMap, OpenStreetMap and Esri. Your photo is used only for the search and is not kept.
